# TRACE-DT on LinkedIn: page content and setup

A note for you, not published with the site. It carries the same information as the website, laid out the LinkedIn way: a Page profile and a feed of posts, rather than menu pages. Research shows CoSeC fellows have used personal profiles and the central CoSeC account rather than project pages, so a dedicated TRACE-DT Page is a small, sensible step that gives the project a home others can follow and reshare.

## A. Which kind of page

Create a **LinkedIn Company Page** of type "Educational institution" or "Research". It is free, it can have several admins, and people follow it without a connection request. You create it from LinkedIn, then paste its address into `linkedin_url` in `_config.yml`.

## B. The profile fields

**Page name**
TRACE-DT

**Tagline (short line under the name, keep it under 120 characters)**
Trustworthy, Real-time and Agentic Digital Twins for Infrastructure. A CoSeC Fellowship at the University of Strathclyde.

**Industry**
Research Services

**About (the overview box, same facts as the website, written for a feed reader)**
TRACE-DT is a CoSeC Fellowship held by Dr Basuraj Bhowmik at the University of Strathclyde, running from October 2026 to December 2027 and funded through STFC Scientific Computing.

Infrastructure now streams data every second, yet the models that read it are often refreshed once a year. TRACE-DT works on digital twins that keep up: twins that update in real time, show how confident they are, and let AI agents take part in decisions while a person stays in charge.

The project brings structural engineers, research software developers, asset owners and policymakers together at an in-person Idea Factory in Glasgow, co-convened with the Institute for Future Cities. The outcome is a co-authored Policy Brief on trustworthy, real-time and agentic digital twins for infrastructure.

Follow for the Idea Factory date, the call to take part, and the Policy Brief when it is published.

## D. Five launch posts

Post these over the first few weeks, newest work at the top.

1. **Launch.** TRACE-DT is live. Over the next fifteen months we are defining what a trustworthy, real-time and agentic digital twin must do for infrastructure, and bringing engineers and research software communities together to build it. A CoSeC Fellowship at the University of Strathclyde. Follow along. #DigitalTwins #CoSeC

2. **The demonstrator.** What does a digital twin actually do? On our site you can drag a truck across a bridge and add damage, and watch the twin re-estimate the natural frequency and raise an alarm. Two minutes, no sign-up. Link in comments. #StructuralHealthMonitoring

3. **The question.** A twin that cannot say how confident it is should not be trusted with a decision about a real structure. Setting that bar, clearly, is the heart of TRACE-DT. What would you need to see before you acted on a twin? #AgenticAI

4. **Idea Factory call.** We are planning an in-person Idea Factory in Glasgow, co-convened with the Institute for Future Cities, for engineers, research software developers, asset owners and policymakers. Register your interest through the link in comments. #Infrastructure

5. **Communities.** TRACE-DT works with the CoSeC communities closest to infrastructure twins, CCP-DCM and CCP-WSI, to turn shared problems into shared software. If that is your field, say hello. #ResearchSoftware

## E. Setup steps

1. Sign in to LinkedIn. Click the **For Business** grid at the top right, then **Create a Company Page**.
2. Choose the Company or Institution type, enter the name TRACE-DT and the public address you want.
3. Paste the tagline, About, industry and specialties from section B.
4. Upload the logo and banner from section C.
5. Click **Create page**, then add a co-admin so the page does not depend on one account.
6. Copy the page address and paste it into `linkedin_url` in `_config.yml` on the website.
7. Publish launch post 1, then space out the rest.
