## Why TRACE-DT

Bridges, wind turbines and buildings now stream data every second. The models that interpret those data are often recalibrated once a year. That gap is where damage goes unnoticed and where decisions rest on stale evidence.

A useful digital twin has to update as the structure changes, explain what it has inferred, and say how confident it is. When AI agents begin to act on that twin, the bar for trust rises again. TRACE-DT is a community effort to set out that bar clearly and to connect it with the research software that can meet it.

## The Idea Factory

The centre of the fellowship is an in-person Idea Factory in Glasgow, co-convened with the Institute for Future Cities. It brings engineers, research software developers, asset owners and policy people into one room to turn shared problems into project ideas. The discussion feeds a co-authored Policy Brief on trustworthy, real-time and agentic digital twins for infrastructure.

Three questions frame the day:

- What evidence must a twin give before an engineer acts on it?
- Where can AI agents safely take part in monitoring and maintenance decisions?
- Which shared software would move the whole field forward?
