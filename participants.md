# Participants

The fellow, the partners who convene and support the work, and the communities it serves.

## Fellow

### Dr Basuraj Bhowmik

![Dr Basuraj Bhowmik](images/people/photo-placeholder.svg){: .portrait}

**Fellow and Principal Investigator.** Lecturer in Structural Health Monitoring, University of Strathclyde, and Director of the OSCAR Lab (Online Structural Control and Monitoring). [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-7782-513X)

His research builds real-time methods that track how structures behave and flag damage as it happens, and extends them into physics-informed digital twins and agentic AI for infrastructure. He leads TRACE-DT and convenes its Idea Factory.

## Idea Factory co-convenor

### Institute for Future Cities

The Institute for Future Cities, University of Strathclyde, co-convenes the TRACE-DT Idea Factory and brings links to city partners and infrastructure owners.
